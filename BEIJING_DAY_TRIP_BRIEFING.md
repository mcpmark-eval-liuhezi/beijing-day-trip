### Practical Tips (from recent visitor reviews)
- **Book tickets in advance.** Daily cap is 40,000 visitors and tickets sell out quickly. Passports are strictly required for entry.
- **Go early.** Reviews consistently recommend being at the gates right at 8:30 AM opening to beat the crowds.
- **Allow several hours** — the complex is enormous (~720,000 m², 980 buildings). Most visitors need 3–4+ hours and don't see everything.
- **Comfortable shoes, water, and snacks** — bring your own; inside refreshments are available but costly.
- **Security checks** at entrances; items like lighters, tripods, selfie sticks, drones, and power banks over 20,000 mAh are banned.
- **Free entry** for seniors over 60.
- **Consider a half-day guided tour** if you're not deep into Chinese history — a guide brings the stories and details to life.

## Suggested One-Day Flow

1. **8:30 AM** — Arrive at the Palace Museum at opening; enter via Tiananmen Square first, then through the Meridian Gate.
2. **9:00 AM – 12:30 PM** — Walk the central axis: Meridian Gate → Tiananmen → Hall of Supreme Harmony → Court of Imperial Labor → Gate of Divine Prowess → exit north at the Palace Museum north gate.
3. **12:30 PM – 1:30 PM** — Lunch nearby (Donghuamen / 果树巷 area has good options).
4. **2:00 PM** — Optional add-on: Jingshan Park (directly north — great panoramic photo of the Forbidden City rooftops) or a short walk through the Imperial Garden.
5. **3:30 PM** — Wrap up before the 4:30 PM closing cutoff; the museum shuts at 4:30 PM so plan to be out by then.

**Bottom line:** Target a **Tuesday–Sunday, 8:30 AM start**, book tickets online a few days ahead, bring passport + comfortable shoes + water, and allow a half day minimum.
